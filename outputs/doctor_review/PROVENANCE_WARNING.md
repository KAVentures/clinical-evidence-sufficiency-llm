# PROVENANCE WARNING — NOT INDEPENDENT PHYSICIAN RATINGS

The public `doctor_review_*.csv`, `adjudication_*.csv`, and
`sensitivity_*.csv` files in this directory are **blank reviewer packets**.
They do not contain independent human annotations.

Earlier completed workbooks and historical analyses were created during
AI-assisted research sessions. Prior conversation records confirm ChatGPT
completed a 120-case review and 72-case sensitivity worksheets in reviewer
styles; multiple completed workbooks in the research library carry
model-generated provenance. A separate independent physician reference
standard could not be authenticated.

**Do not treat the archived `adjudication_report_final.json`,
`sensitivity_report.json`, or `sensitivity_report_excludeC.json` as clinical
calibration, physician consensus, sensitivity, or specificity.** These were
removed from scientific findings in the revised manuscript and are retained
only for transparent historical provenance. Any future human rating study
requires actual clinician recruitment, immutable original labels and
documented review procedures.
