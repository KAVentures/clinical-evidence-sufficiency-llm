# Research ethics and data-provenance self-assessment

**Study:** *Judge-dependent safety gains and model-specific helpfulness costs of evidence-sufficiency prompting in clinical LLMs*  
**Author:** Koyar Afrasyab, MD; Kinvectum AB, Sweden  
**Study type:** Retrospective computational benchmark using deidentified/public-source case materials and machine-generated model responses.

## Participant and reference-standard status

No patient was recruited, contacted, treated, or exposed to an intervention.
No identifiable patient record was intentionally collected for the benchmark.
Real-POCQi, HealthBench, and MedRBench remain subject to their respective
data-use and citation terms; MIMIC-derived data were excluded from external
API execution.

**No independently verified physician judging panel was conducted for the
scientific analyses reported in this revised manuscript.** Earlier internal
files labeled as blinded physician evaluations cannot be authenticated as
human observations. Several completed workbooks were generated by AI during
prior research sessions; prior instructions show AI directly filled 120-case
and 72-case reviewer ratings. These are not clinical labels or independent
expert judgment, regardless of physician-style prompts. They are excluded
from inference, and the historical reports are flagged as deprecated.

The physician author designed and interpreted the study but does not thereby
turn AI-written reviewer worksheets into a physician reference standard.

## Ethical review

The investigator assessed this non-interventional computational benchmark as
outside mandatory review under the Swedish Ethical Review Act (2003:460),
given the absence of direct human research intervention or identifiable
sensitive health data from enrolled persons. No ethics approval or formal
exemption decision was requested or obtained. This is a self-assessment, not
a binding institutional/legal determination.

Any subsequent genuine clinician-rating study must separately evaluate
recruitment, consent, compensation, data processing, professional information,
and applicable institutional or ethics-review requirements.

## Audit and privacy

The original historical model-generated sheets should be retained in a
controlled provenance archive, not misrepresented as blinded doctors' labels
or merged into the public benchmark's human reference set. Public outputs
should contain no personal identities, provider credentials, or confidential
patient-source text beyond permitted dataset licences.

## Scientific boundary

The present results identify prompt-condition differences **under specified
AI evaluators**, not clinical harm prevalence, diagnostic-device performance,
or readiness for autonomous care. Independent external clinical validation
has not been established.

**Status:** research investigator self-assessment; no formal authority determination.
